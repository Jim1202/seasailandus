260819 ALCYONE/2026 SEASON... WITH GORDON N FIONA (22 DAYS FROM AMANU TO PAPEETE)

We’ve known Gordon and Fiona for more than 20 years. This Scottish couple is absolutely crazy about sailing. They live in Hong Kong and regularly race at the famous RHKYC, where Fiona worked for many years.

Our sons Jim and Calum are best friends and have a great sailing pedigree, having raced extensively in Optimists and 420s, with dreams at one point of representing Hong Kong in the 470 at the Olympics. Their second son, Duncan, is now preparing for the next Olympics in the Nacra.

Even though we are planning to sell ALCYONE this summer, we couldn’t miss the opportunity to have them on board for one last AMANU safari before sailing on to Tahiti. 

Thanks to the people of the atoll to have preserved up to nom their piece of paradise! 

                  God save AMANU !
