261113 THE SALES ARE OVER: ALCYONE BACK HOME: WHANGAREI



Tks to Starlink...i can continue the blog on board with this new post 

1)Why planning a return to Whangārei ?

=> ALCYONE has been for sale on her Tahaa mooring for three months, but the market is not at its best. Our broker Eric, after long discussions, started receiving crazy offers from November 1st, the official beginning of the cyclone season. Specialists are also insisting on the exceptional El Niño effect expected this year in French Polynesia.

=> There was no realistic way to get ALCYONE out of this dangerous area: our friends in Amanu cannot leave home at such short notice, Bertrand, my former partner, is still in Kauehi, and a professional crew cannot sail her with an out-of-date life raft.

NB: Crazy situation — we bought a top-quality WAYPOINT OCEAN life raft in Auckland, and nobody can service or recertify it in Raiatea. And the cost of having it done elsewhere is almost as high as buying a new one. Better not to touch this raft!

=> Two pieces of very good news on Friday, November 13th: Helena's health is no longer a “day-by-day” situation, giving us much more visibility for the longer term, and Jim suddenly got lucky with a vacancy from Madrid.

=> This gives us a small window of opportunity to try our luck and make the trip before Christmas. It is also possible because ALCYONE was ready to leave her mooring, with all tanks full and everything on board.


2) 15-16 november: RUSH TO RAIATEA 

The air plane one may ticket is bought immediatly and l arrive in PAPEETE on 16 to meet quickly Eric in airport and Fred my boat care in RAIATEA.

            this Nino season is starting with very strong SE winds

With such short notice, Fred somehow found a way to pick me up at the airport and drove me in his “limousine” to Carrefour to buy some basic supplies — there is no way to get food at the Tahaa mooring — before finally reaching ALCYONE.
Even GERONIMO, Olivier de KERSAUSON's famous trimaran must find a safe place to spend for the heart of the Nino season
 

    Taxi boat to cross the windy lagoon to TAHAA "APU bay "


Hi ALCYONE again after 3 month: Tks to the weekly boat care of Fred (bms), she can sail again immediatly if we rig back the jib.  

3) 17-18 november Couple of days to prepare
   
Easy to organise, as the cat was almost ready to leave. I only needed to top up the diesel (4 × 20 litres), water (8 × 10 litres), and fresh food. The real challenge was buying everything and getting it all back to ALCYONE… only possible in Polynesia!

Fred's famous RAIATEA limousine is ready
   
Market as all you need is very closed to the dock in UTUROA


Lucien and Jean Luc: help is always a smile away in Polynesia

Bonus: top seasonal fruits for next sailing  




4) CROSSING TAHAA TO WHANGAREI

19 november: Instant start 12h00 from APU BAY gusting 30k: 
99m before midnight


 
Morning situation: without jib, difficult to imgine that i ll leave APU bay at lunch time

After rigging the jib between gust, time is to worry about the mooring

JIMBOE unturning the 10+ turns around the mooring lines



12h30 2nd reef ok,  JIMBOE jump on his windsurfboard and ALCYONE is on her way

No minute to waste: December has 2 times more chance to get a cyclone than November !

"Farewell RAIATEA"  Cool start without rain and with protection of the island 





No sleep tonight with 30k: 2 reef  + 3 rolls on jib with windvane 100-120.


20 november: 2 reefs with jib 3 rolls ...Another sport's day
                          230m.   0h position 17 09 3. -153 04 8
   Alcyone downwind, surfing to fast in a mine field more than 15k

One of these invite in the cockpit when i try by mistake to adjust the pilot to level 9 

 

21 november: wind ease, sun is back, n a lot of sleep
210m     0h position 18 34 3. 156 47 9
                

One of the 2 bonus of this trip: 

With the sun back, time to enjoy the second bonus: our new, safe cockpit shower!


22 november: easy day with all sails out (no code 0 as bowsprit) 

                 194m      0h position 210 06 6. 159 53 1


Speed below 7k ...let's lift up the main sail until evening !

 
The nightmare: West wind on NZ arrival...but  forecast more than 3 days are weak !




23 november : Bad news from NZ n another jumping night

                    205m     0h position 22 57. 166 18 4

   Last sun from the day..wind ll pick up a bit too much south... 


 
Rain + SW 20-25 on the way for the night, Have to bear away 20d from the rhumb line 


Situation today: a true barrier before NZ



Situation next week in NZ... a bit of motivation to rush !



24 novembre: no more windmeter, another active night 
232m      0h position 22 56 4. 166 18 7

3rd reef before the night to calm down a bit 

Old fashion panholder: what else ?

Indoor control from skipper berth with ST600R

South swell, wind... n rain... daggerboards full up !


      No choice: Alcyone has to sail 2 days through the "red district" !

2 waves invite themselves in the cockpit , but not welcome indoor !





Bright moonlight, and from midnight onward, both the wind and the sea gradually ease.



25 november: Easing, but with squalls

ALCYONE left the bad grib zone (25-30k) around midnight

Alcyone must reach Whangarei before that !
