Overview



ALCYONE is a 2004 Outremer 55 Light, one of the last units built before the passing of the founder and designer Gérard Danson.

With only around thirty units produced, this model is widely recognized as the best design of the first-generation Outremer range: fast, strong, and extremely reliable.

This is our second 55 Light, after PAPOOSE, on which we completed a full family circumnavigation (1998–2005). This experience gave us a deep understanding of the boat and its capabilities.

Purchased in New Zealand in 2017 (3rd owner), ALCYONE has been carefully refitted and optimized before sailing again across the Pacific.

Since 2021, she has been based in French Polynesia, on the remote atoll of Amanu, and continuously maintained with permanent onboard presence.
