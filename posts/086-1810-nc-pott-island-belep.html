1810 NC POTT ISLAND BELEP

 We continue to extrem north of Pott island; no need to take the risk to enter in the north inlet with this strong wind n average clarity of water as anchoring in the leeward of the coast offer enough protection. All spots are a dinghy ride away. 






nice walks...

poor shelling,

n good crab hunting






Inlet bottom with new mangrove growing in typical NewCal red mud
